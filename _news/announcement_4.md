Thrilled to announce that I've been selected as a 2026 [Schmidt Sciences Humanities and AI Virtual Institute (HAVI) Development Award](https://www.schmidtsciences.org/humanities-and-ai-virtual-institute/) recipient! I'll be leading "Unlocking Hidden Histories: Adapting Handwritten Text Recognition for Colonial Paraguayan Archives" in collaboration with Professor Patricia Murrieta-Flores (Tecnológico de Monterrey / Lancaster University). This project will adapt AI technology to recover the voices of enslaved Indigenous and African descent people from colonial Paraguayan manuscripts—transforming 15-20 years of manual transcription work into months through cutting-edge HTR models.
