Thrilled to announce I've been invited to present papers and chair a roundtable on Indigenous History at the VI Congresso Internacional de História Indígena (COIMI), taking place in Salvador, Bahia, Brazil, from February 25th to 28th, 2026 — one of the most important international gatherings dedicated to Indigenous History in Latin America!
I'll be contributing to vital scholarly conversations on Indigenous resistance, colonial dispossession, and decolonial methodologies, bringing perspectives from my research on colonial Paraguay and Guaraní history.
Watch my invitation video to find out more about my presentations and the roundtable I'll be chairing — and I warmly encourage you to join us in Salvador!
