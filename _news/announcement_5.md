I have been invited as a keynote speaker and to co-chair a roundtable on Indigenous People and Religious Missions in the Abya Yala at the VI Congresso Internacional de História Indígena (COIMI) [VI Congresso Internacional de História Indígena (COIMI)](https://coimi.net/pt/), taking place in Salvador, Bahia, Brazil, from February 25th to 28th, 2026, one of the most important international gatherings dedicated to Indigenous History in Latin America!
I'll be contributing to vital scholarly and intercultural conversations on Indigenous resistance, colonial dispossession, and decolonial methodologies, drawing on my research on colonial Paraguay, Guaraní history and AI-Digital Humanities.
Watch my invitation video to find out more about my presentations and the roundtable I'll be co-chairing with Dr Carlos Daniel Paz, and I warmly encourage you to join us in Salvador!
